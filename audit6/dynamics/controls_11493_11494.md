## 0. Net and rail names used below

| Name | Meaning | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **SC0V** | Sidechain 0 V (drawn as a heavy line on EX11494) | 11494 CONN 3.7 "SIDECHAIN I/P 0V". It is distributed as FSP6.2 (to 11495) and FSP3.2 (to 11493). [M-H] |
| **+2.45V / -2.45V** | Sidechain reference rails | They enter 11494 on FSP6.3/6.4 (from 11495) and pass to 11493 on FSP3.3/3.4. [H for labels] |
| **-4.9V** | Gate range reference | Comes from 11495 on FSP4.9 (on 11493). [H for label] |
| **0VL / -15VL** | Logic 0 V and -15 V | Used for the switch "logic" levels sent to 11495. [M] |
| **-15VR** | Recall rail | Feeds the "a" gangs of the pots (recall read-back only). [H] |

All dynamics pots are dual 10 K. The "b" gang is the audio/CV gang. The "a" gang is a recall potentiometer between -15VR and 0 V whose wiper goes to CONN 12/13 (11494) or CONN 14/15 (11493) through a 100R / 33µ/16V / 100R filter. The "a" gangs do not affect the audio and are ignored below except where noted.

## 1. Front-panel controls

### 1A. Panel legends (photo 20.09.23 (3).jpeg, cross-checked with the 1.0 p4 drawing)

The dynamics section sits **below the HPF/LPF filters and above the AUX sends**. There are two columns.

**Left column, GATE/EXPANDER (board 11493):**
- **KEY** button with a yellow LED.
- **INV** button with a yellow LED.
- **HYST** knob, black cap. Legends: `EXP` (boxed) at about 1 o'clock, `5` at 3, `10` at 5, a `⊥` tick at 6, `15` at 7, `20` at 9, `25` at 11.
- **THR** knob, grey cap. Legends: `-25` at 1, `-17` at 3, `-10` at 5 (partly hidden), `⊥` at 6, `0` at 7, `+7` at 9, `+15` at 11.

- **RNGE** knob, yellow cap. Legends: `0` at 7 (hidden in the photo, present on the drawing), `10` at 9, `20` at 11, a `⊤` tick at 12, `30` at 1, `40` at 3, and about `60` at 5 (partly hidden: "…0" in the photo, "00/60" on the drawing).

- **REL** knob, black cap. Legends: `0.03` (CCW), `0.3` (top), `3s` (CW).
- **GATE** button (in/out) with a green LED.

**Right column, LIMITER/COMPRESSOR (board 11494):**
- **L/C** button with a green LED.
- **→** button (the stereo LINK, drawn as an arrow) with a red LED.
- **GAIN** knob, red cap. Legends: `0` at 1, `6` at 3, `12` at 5, `⊥` at 6, `18` at 7, `24` at 9, `30` at 11.
- **THR** knob, grey cap. Legends: `+20` at 1, `+14` at 3, `+8` at 5, `⊥` at 6, `+2` at 7, `-4` at 9, `-10` at 11.
  - Bracket legend `pull -20`.
- **RAT** knob, orange cap. Legends: `1` at 7, `2` at 9, `3` at 12, `5` at 3, `lim` at 5 (the drawing reads "1 RAT lim").
  - Bracket legend `pull FAST`.
- **REL** knob, black cap. Legends: `0.03` (CCW), `0.3`, `3s`, with an **AUTO** box at the CW end.

**How to read the scales [M].**
- RNGE, RAT and REL are conventional 300° scales: the stops are at the bottom, with CCW at 7 o'clock and CW at 5 o'clock.
- HYST, both THR knobs and GAIN only read monotonically if the end-stops are at 12 o'clock. The `⊥` mark at 6 o'clock would then be the mid-scale tick.
- Read that way:
  - **gate THR** runs -25 (CCW) to +15 (CW);
  - **L/C THR** runs +20 (CCW) to -10 (CW);
  - **GAIN** runs 0 to 30 dB CW;
  - **HYST** runs EXP (CCW) and then 5 to 25 dB CW.
- The schematic supports this. The hysteresis expand switch is labelled "CCW-EXPAND", and the two THR pots are wired with opposite senses (see 1B and 1C).
- **Check this on the real module before relying on it.**

Knob caps (PL11493 / PL11494 list KA175xx caps; colours match the photo): grey/white (THR), black/white (REL, HYST), red/white (GAIN), yellow/black (RNGE), orange/black (RAT). [H]

Pot part numbers from 1.0 p4 and PL11494 [M-H]:
- **PT45016**: 2×10K 10%, no switch. Used for GAIN RV3 (11494), and probably RELEASE and HYST on 11493.
- **PT45019**: 2×10K plus a pull switch. Used for THR RV4 and RATIO RV5 (11494), and RNGE and THR on 11493.
- **PT45022**: 2×10K "20%+10%" with a **CW switch**. Used for L/C RELEASE RV6 (11494), giving AUTO.
- **PT45021**: appears in the 11493 column. It is probably HYST with its CCW switch. **Not confirmed:** the PL11493 page listing the pots and resistors is missing from the PDF (p3-4 hold only the switch, transistor, cap, LED and knob items).
- **Taper:** not stated for any dynamics pot. Mic-sens and HPF are explicitly "REVLOG" and "VLAW", so the dynamics pots are presumably **linear** [M].

## 2. CV summing, make-up, link, sidechain, in/out switching

### 2A. VCA control-voltage inputs
The VCA (11495 IC12, dbx 2151 per the task) receives three CVs on FSP6. 11495 sums them in IC14 (p17) [H]:
- **FSP6.11 "L/C VC"**: driven by **S3d common**. With **L/C in** it is **FSP6.10 "L/C O/P"**, the compressor sidechain CV output from 11495, passed straight back. With L/C out it is **SC0V**. [H]
- **FSP6.13 "GAIN"**: driven by **S3c common**. With **L/C in** it is the IC9a buffer of RV3 (0 to +2.45 V). With L/C out it is **SC0V**. [H]
- **FSP6.5 "GATE VC"**: comes from 11493 on FSP3.5 (this net is in the p7/p8 gap). It is driven by **11493 S5c common**:
  - **GATE out:** the at-rest contact is SC0V.
  - **GATE in:** the S4c output, that is FSP4.2 GATE O/P, or FSP4.3 INV O/P when INV is pressed.
  - [M: the S5c common's link to FSP3.5 is inferred because the joining segment falls in the scan gap. The contacts themselves are H.]
- The **summing resistor values and scale factors are on 11495** (not on these boards). On these boards the CVs are passed straight through with no series resistance, apart from the 33R/10µH link network in 2C.

### 2B. Dynamics in/out and the audio path through the VCA (one VCA shared by L/C and gate) [H]
- **DYN I/P** comes from the motherboard on 11494 CONN 3.3/3.4. It goes **directly to FSP6.6/6.7 "VCA I/P"** (to 11495) and also to the at-rest contact of S3f.
- **11494 S3f (L/C):** its common feeds FSP5.1 "L/C TO GATE SW" (to 11493).
  - L/C out: VCA I/P, which is a bypass.
  - L/C in: FSP6.8 "VCA O/P".
- **11493 S5f (GATE):** its common is FSP5.3 "DYN O/P".
  - Gate out: FSP5.1 (the S3f result).
  - Gate in: FSP5.5 "VCA O/P".
- **DYN O/P** returns on 11494 FSP5.3 to **CONN 3.9/3.10 "DYN O/P"** (to the motherboard).
- So the VCA always processes the dynamics input. Its output is used if **either** L/C or GATE is in, and the unused section's CV is held at SC0V.
- The **0 V legs** (VCA I/P 0V, VCA O/P 0V, DYN O/P 0V) are switched in parallel by S3e and S5e, with **R50/R51 100R (11494)** and **R13/R14 100R (11493)** as 0-V ground-lift resistors. Exact contact mapping is M. This is not needed for a behavioural model.
- **Position in the channel path:** not set on these boards. DYN I/P and DYN O/P go through motherboard CONN 3, and the order is selected elsewhere. The panel has **PREQ** (yellow LED) and **DYN** (red LED) buttons next to INS on the fader/routing board. 1.5 p3 lists "Dynamics DYN S6" on Mini Fader Board 11498. PREQ is presumably dynamics pre-EQ. **Not traced; see EX11498/11499.**
- **Signal level at the VCA input:** not determinable from these boards. There is no gain stage between CONN 3.3 and FSP6.6 on 11494, so the level is whatever the motherboard routes in (channel line level after the input stage/filters, or after EQ). **Unknown.**

### 2C. LINK (→) between adjacent channels [M-H]
- **FSP6.10 "L/C O/P"** feeds a parallel **R54 33R ∥ L2 10µH**, ending at a link node.
- The link node is **hard-wired to CONN 4.5 "LOOP I/P"**.
- **S4d (LINK)**: the common is **CONN 4.4 "LOOP O/P"**.
  - Pressed: it connects to the link node (and so to LOOP I/P).
  - At rest: its contact is an unconnected stub.
- If the motherboard wires each channel's LOOP O/P to the next channel's LOOP I/P (assumption), then pressing → on channel N ties the L/C sidechain outputs of N and N+1 together through 33R‖10µH + 33R‖10µH.
- The VCA of each channel still takes its own L/C O/P (S3d is fed from FSP6.10 directly, ahead of R54). So the coupling happens at the 11495 sidechain output node, where the dominant (larger) gain reduction presumably wins.
- Only the **compressor** CV is linked. The gate is not linked on these boards.
- R96 7K5 1% via S4b goes to the recall status.
- The label "…LINK" next to FSP3 is cut off by the missing strip.

### 2D. Sidechain/key sources [H unless noted]
- **L/C sidechain:** **11494 CONN 3.6 "SIDECHAIN I/P"** goes to FSP6.1 "L/C I/P" (11495) and FSP3.1 (11493). There is **no selection on these boards**: the compressor sidechain is always this motherboard signal, and its reference is CONN 3.7 → SC0V.
  - R48 100R joins a line-input 0V node (CONN 1.8 area) toward the sidechain-0V/"B" net [L].
- **Gate sidechain:** 11493 S3c (KEY) selects L/C I/P or **KEY O/P**.
- **KEY O/P (FSP2.12)** is an external key input on 11494 CONN 4.1 (HI) and 4.2 (LO):
  - **IC9 pins 12-14** form a differential amplifier.
  - HI goes through R58 7K5 into +, with R59‖R60 (7K5+7K5 = 3K75) to CONN 3.7 (sidechain 0V).
  - LO goes through R57 15K into −, with R61 7K5 feedback.
  - Gain is **-6 dB differential (×0.5)**. Input impedance is about 11K25 (HI) and 15K (LO).
  - The output goes through **C28 10µ/25V**, then R?8 220K to the "F" net (the first digit is unreadable; probably sidechain 0V), and then to FSP2.12.

## 3. Meter and LED drive

- **COMPRESS METER** (11494 CONN 4.9): IC9 (TL074) pin 10 is buffered to pin 8, then **R56 33R** feeds CONN 4.9. The input is the **S3d common**, which is the L/C VC (L/C O/P when L/C is in, SC0V when out). [H for topology; the CONN pin numbers come from the CONN 4 legend, because the IC9 output arrows are cut off by the missing strip.]
- **EXPAND METER** (11494 CONN 4.10): IC9 pin 5 is buffered to pin 7, then **R55 33R** feeds CONN 4.10. The input is **GATE VC (FSP3.5)**, so the meter reads the gate/expander gain-reduction CV after the GATE and INV switching (0 when the gate is out). [H]
- Both meter drives are the raw VCA control voltages, unscaled. Scaling is done on meter board **11637** (book1 6.1 p18-21):
  - Inverting buffers (TL064) with offset trims (RV11/RV12) feed LM3914/LM3915 bargraph drivers.
  - The parts list suggests LM3914 (lin) for the L/C chain (IC12…) and LM3915 (log) for the expander chain (IC13…) [L].
- **There is no "gate open/closed" LED on 11493 or 11494.** Gate activity is shown only on the EXPAND meter. [M-H]
- **LED strings:** both boards use a series LED chain fed from a constant-current source elsewhere ("TO LED 2A" … "LED 1K"). Each LED is shorted by a switch section or a PNP transistor when it should be off.
  - 11494: LED3 GREEN = L/C in (S3a); LED4 RED = LINK (S4a); LED5 RED = PULL -20 (TR8).
  - 11493: LED4 YEL = KEY (S3b); LED5 YEL = INV (S4b); LED6 RED = PULL -30 (TR3); LED7 GREEN = GATE in (S5b).
